What is numerology?

Numerology is the study of the symbolic meaning of numbers and how they relate to our lives. It turns two things that are uniquely yours, the name you were given at birth and your date of birth, into a small set of numbers. Each number describes a different part of you: your path in life, your natural talents, your inner motivations and the way others see you.

The most widely used method today is the Pythagorean system, named after the Greek mathematician Pythagoras, who taught that numbers underlie everything in the universe. In it, every letter has a value from 1 to 9, and every number is reduced to a single digit, except the special master numbers 11, 22 and 33.

Example: the Life Path number

For a birth date of 15 July 1990, reduce the month, day and year separately, then add them together:

  1. Month: July = 7
  2. Day: 15 → 1 + 5 = 6
  3. Year: 1990 → 1 + 9 + 9 + 0 = 19 → 1 + 9 = 10 → 1 + 0 = 1
  4. Total: 7 + 6 + 1 = 14 → 1 + 4 = Life Path 5

Your name numbers work the same way: add the values of the letters in your full birth name (all letters for Expression, vowels for Soul Urge, consonants for Personality) and reduce the total.

Master numbers 11, 22 and 33

When a total reaches 11, 22 or 33 along the way, it isn't reduced further. These master numbers carry the qualities of their single digit (2, 4 and 6) in a stronger, more demanding form: 11 is the intuitive messenger, 22 the master builder and 33 the master teacher.
