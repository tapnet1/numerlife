What we collect

To prepare your report we ask for your full birth name, date of birth and place of birth. To send it, we ask for your email address and, if you choose, your phone number.

How we use it

We use these details to calculate your numbers and send you your report. If you reached us through a numerologist's personal link, your details and report are shared with that numerologist so they can follow up with you. We only send you insights and offers if you tick the separate box for that, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Our follow-up emails record whether they were opened and which links were clicked, so we can tell which emails are useful and send fewer of the ones that aren't. This is linked to your email address and stops when you unsubscribe.

If you buy a full written reading, you pay through PayPal. PayPal handles your payment details; we receive only a confirmation, the amount and the email address PayPal shares with us, which we keep with your order.

Some numerologists use other tools in their sessions (for example a Healy device). With your agreement, your numerologist can copy your name, birth date, contact details and address to their own device for that purpose. If they record your home address for this, it is kept on {{ site_name }} with the rest of your details until you ask us to delete it.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information. We store your connection's address only in a scrambled form, to protect the site from abuse, and delete those records after about a day.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by replying to your report email or contacting the numerologist who referred you.
