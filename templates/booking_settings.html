Your calendar (optional)

Paste your calendar's private link and anything you're busy with there (appointments, family, holidays) can't be booked. numer.life checks it every few minutes and only keeps when you're busy, never what the event is. It can't change your calendar: add bookings with "Add to my calendar" on Appointments.

Where to find the link
  1. Google Calendar (on a computer): Settings ⚙ → click your calendar under "Settings for my calendars" → Integrate calendar → copy Secret address in iCal format. Done when the link ends in basic.ics.
  2. Apple iCloud: in Calendar, share the calendar → turn on Public Calendar → copy the link (starts with webcal://).
  3. Outlook: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar → "Can view when I'm busy" → copy the ICS link.
